What does the 06-month testing warranty cover?add

Used components that pass our 24-hour bench test are covered for six months against functional failure under normal use. Brand-new items carry store or agent warranty depending on the product.

Are the prices final?add

Listed prices are our current retail prices in LKR. Stock and market movement can change availability, so we always confirm stock and your final total on WhatsApp before you pay.

Do you deliver outside Embilipitiya?add

Yes. We ship island-wide using ESD-safe packaging and trusted couriers. You can also collect in person from our Embilipitiya workshop.

Can I mix used and brand-new items in one order?add

Absolutely. Your quote list is shared across both divisions, so you can combine them and send a single WhatsApp order.

Can you assemble a PC for me?add

Yes — choose "Workshop Assembly" in your quote or message us. Our technicians fit, cable-manage and stress-test the full build.
